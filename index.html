🔑 Spare Key

If you build or look after websites for other people, this helps you make sure they are not stuck if you are.

Make a handover for a client

Add the client’s domains and Spare Key looks up where their website, email and domain settings live and when things renew. Fill in who owns and pays for each part. It then shows what would break if you were unavailable, and writes a handover document for the client to keep.

  • No passwords. Never type one in. Record where they are kept instead.
  • Nothing stored on a server. Only domain names are sent, to look up public records. Your draft stays in this browser until you save the file.
  • No account, cookies or tracking.
